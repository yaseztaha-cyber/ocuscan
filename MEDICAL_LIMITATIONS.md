# OcuScan Medical Limitations (v0.5)

Read this before interpreting any result. Screening observations are
not diagnoses; a normal screen never means healthy; a flag never names
a disease or its cause.

- A phone camera cannot see: retina, lens interior, optic nerve,
  intraocular pressure, refraction values, blood values, nerve function.
- Many diseases are invisible from outside; findings have multiple
  causes; the app cannot determine which applies.
- Thresholds are prototype engineering values, not clinical diagnostic
  cutoffs, unless a cited source states otherwise.
- Performance varies across skin tones, eye colors, ages, glasses,
  devices, lighting — unevaluated groups are labeled "Not evaluated",
  never assumed equal. AI trained mostly on high-income-country data
  may not transfer; OcuScan has no validated performance anywhere yet.
- Vision tests are screening measurements with operator-maintained
  conditions — never prescriptions; color/contrast depend on the
  specific display; peripheral screening is not perimetry; cover testing
  is observer-reported, not clinician testing.
- Pupil light-response testing is disabled without a controlled safe
  stimulus. No flash is ever used.
- Retinal screening needs fundus optics no phone has; selfie images are
  rejected for retinal analysis by design.
- Neurological research tracks (palsy, Parkinsonian, ALS, dementia,
  voice, gait) are directions with protocols — no classifiers, no
  measurements, no findings in this build.
- Emergency symptoms must go to urgent care, never to the camera.
