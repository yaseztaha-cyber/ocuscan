Effective date: 2026-10-12. Version 1.0 (matches app 0.7.22+).

## Short version

OcuScan works **fully on your device**. It does not create accounts,
does not run analytics, does not show ads, and does not upload your
images, measurements, or results anywhere. What you share leaves the
phone only when **you** tap a share/export button.

## Data we process — and where it stays

- **Camera images.** Frames are analyzed in the phone's memory to
  locate eyes and take measurements. Raw frames are deleted by
  default; a still you capture is kept only if you enable raw-image
  storage in Settings, and only on the phone.
- **Measurements and history.** Screening observations, trends, and
  person profiles you create are stored in a database **on the phone
  only**. Android backup of app data is disabled, so uninstalling the
  app deletes everything.
- **Diagnostics (optional).** Error/tech reports contain counts,
  timings, and error text — never images. They leave the phone only
  through the system share sheet, which you control.

## Data we never collect

No account identifiers, no location, no contacts, no microphone
audio (the app never requests or uses the microphone), no advertising
identifiers, no crash-reporting or analytics SDKs. The app makes no
network requests for its own features; it works in airplane mode.

Note: the on-device vision libraries (ML Kit, CameraX) are system
components that may emit their own aggregated SDK telemetry through
Google Play services according to Google's policies. OcuScan itself
sends nothing.

## Sharing and deletion

- Report/CSV/JSON exports and error reports are created as local
  files and shared **only** when you choose Share.
- The health report screen has no copy button, so results are never
  placed on the clipboard by the app.
- Settings → Delete removes local results, sessions, research data,
  and reference labels.

## Children

The app is a general screening-support tool with no age gate. A
parent or guardian should supervise use by children.

## Medical role

OcuScan is a screening-support and education tool. It does not
diagnose disease, does not replace an eye examination, and its
observations are not medical advice. Emergencies belong to emergency
care, not to this app.
